VISTA evaluation results

Hypotheses

Each verdict is computed from the result CSVs by scripts/build_dashboard.py. A verdict is supported when the measured effect goes in the hypothesised direction on this workload. The workload is synthetic, so none of this is a real-world prevalence claim.

Disclosure and decision quality

Leakage per accepted payment (H2)

Surprisal −log₂ π(X ≥ disclosed profile) under the population prior. BOP is the information floor −log₂ π(Φ = 1).

Agreement with the receiver's own policy (H1)

How often each approach reaches the decision the receiver's policy would reach on the sender's true evidence.

Selection channel and cumulative leakage

Selection leakage vs cost overhead (E12)

Toy space from blueprint §21. DET is deterministic minimum-cost selection. EXP is the exponential mechanism. LP is the cost-optimal ε-private kernel. BOP proves every branch and leaks nothing through selection.

Threshold probing against the ledger (E14)

Bits an adaptive receiver learns about a 128-value attribute. The ledger caps it at log₂(128/ρ).

Cumulative leakage over repeated payments (E16)

Branch-oblivious proof cost (E13)

Generate + verify, median. CDS OR-proofs over a 2048-bit group; cost grows linearly with the number of branches.

Throughput scaling (E7)

Fail-closed under injected faults (E10)

Share of satisfiable payments accepted each minute. Shaded bands: registry outage (status goes stale after 5 minutes), revoked issuer key until rotation, and schema rollout skew.

Latency by component (E7)

CPU-path components are measured. Rail latency is sampled from a lognormal (median 12 ms).
