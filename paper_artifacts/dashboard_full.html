Selection channel and cumulative leakage

Selection leakage vs cost overhead (E12)

Toy space from blueprint §21. DET is deterministic minimum-cost selection. EXP is the exponential mechanism. LP is the cost-optimal ε-private kernel. BOP proves every branch and leaks nothing through selection.

Threshold probing against the ledger (E14)

Bits an adaptive receiver learns about a 128-value attribute. The ledger caps it at log₂(128/ρ).

Cumulative leakage over repeated payments (E16)

Branch-oblivious proof cost (E13)

Generate + verify, median. CDS OR-proofs over a 2048-bit group; cost grows linearly with the number of branches.

Selection-leakage theory and the disclosure governor (E23–E28)

Leakage–cost frontier, skewed prior (E24)

PCGS is the exact minimum of Λ at each expected cost (Theorem 2). RAND is the exponential mechanism, LP the cost-optimal ε-private kernel. No kernel goes below the floor, and the floor is above zero for every policy with two or more incomparable branches under a full-support prior (Theorem 1).

Selection leakage over repeated payments (E25)

Exact Λₙ with unchanged evidence on 64-state toy spaces (three- and four-branch policies). Fresh draws let the receiver collect the supported-branch set; sticky selection reveals no more than the first payment (Theorem 3).

Branch-inference attacker on the payment workload (E25)

Accuracy above the prior-only baseline of an attacker trained on half of the payers and tested on the other half, by position in the (payer, receiver, policy) sequence. Evidence drifts between payments as in the workload.

Stale-status grace during a registry outage (E28)

Micro and standard tiers may use a status snapshot up to τ = −ln(1−ε)/λᵣ old; high-value tiers stay fail-closed.

Positive association on login signals (E26)

TP2: adjacent 2×2 log odds ratios (the pairwise FKG lattice condition). PQD: P(Xᵢ≥a, Xⱼ≥b) ≥ P(Xᵢ≥a)P(Xⱼ≥b). A violation counts when it is below zero by more than 3 standard errors.

Integrated disclosure governor, one component at a time (E27)

Exposure-weighted Λ charges each payment the Λₙ of its position in its (payer, receiver, policy) sequence under the unchanged-evidence model. Ledger false rejects are satisfiable payments the interval ledger refused.
